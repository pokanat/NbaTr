About the numbers

Over and Under rates describe how often a team scored above or below the selected line in its recent completed preseason games. They are not probabilities or guarantees for future matches. Preseason rotations, roster changes and small samples limit predictive value.

Data from ESPN's public NBA scoreboard endpoint, subject to availability. Scores are not fabricated when data cannot be fetched. This site does not place bets.
